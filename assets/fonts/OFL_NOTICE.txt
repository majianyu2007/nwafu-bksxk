The bundled fonts NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf and NotoSansSC-Medium.ttf are subsets of
Noto Sans SC by Google, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). They have been instanced to a single weight and
subset to the GB2312 character set plus every character that appears in the
school's course catalogue (course, teacher, department and room names), Latin,
CJK punctuation and common symbols, for offline use. No other modification.
